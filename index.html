Welcome. No experience needed.

This app shows you one note at a time: which string and where to press. It then listens through your microphone. When you play the right note, the light turns green and you move on.

The strings

Strings are numbered 1 to 6. String 1 is the thinnest (closest to the floor when you hold the guitar). String 6 is the thickest (closest to your face).

From thickest to thinnest their names are E A D G B e. A memory aid: "Eddie Ate Dynamite, Good Bye Eddie".

Frets

The metal bars across the neck are frets. "Fret 3" means: press the string with a fingertip in the gap between the 2nd and 3rd bar, close to the 3rd bar. "Fret 0" (or "open") means you press nothing and just pluck the string.

Press with the very tip of your finger, firmly. A buzzing sound usually means you need to press harder or move closer to the bar.

Fingers

Your fretting-hand fingers are numbered: index = 1, middle = 2, ring = 3, pinky = 4. A good starting rule: fret 1 → finger 1, fret 2 → finger 2, fret 3 → finger 3.

Reading tab

Songs are written in tab. It has 6 lines, one for each string, and a number on a line tells you which fret to press. The top line is the thinnest string, which is upside-down from what you see when you look down at your guitar. Read from left to right:

e |--0--1--3--------
B |-----------1--3--
G |-----------------
D |-----------------
A |-----------------
E |-----------------

That means: thinnest string open, then fret 1, then fret 3, then the 2nd string at fret 1, then fret 3.

How to use the app

  1. Click Turn on microphone at the top and allow access. Use a quiet room with the guitar near the computer.
  2. Pluck a string. The level bar should jump past the black line. If it doesn't, raise Sensitivity. If it reacts to background noise, lower it.
  3. Tune the guitar first in the Tuner tab. An out-of-tune guitar will never turn the light green.
  4. Go through the Lessons in order, then try the Songs. Click 🔊 Hear it any time to hear how a note should sound. The mic pauses while the app plays.

Sore fingertips in the first weeks are normal. 10–15 minutes every day beats one long session a week.
